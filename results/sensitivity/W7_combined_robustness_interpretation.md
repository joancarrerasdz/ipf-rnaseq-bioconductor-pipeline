# W7 combined robustness interpretation

## Scope

This report integrates the frozen continuous-age and gender sensitivity analyses of the frozen W5 IPF-vs-NDC differential-expression result.

No new differential-expression model was fitted.

The frozen W5 primary analysis remains the reference analysis.

## Continuous-age sensitivity

Continuous-age adjustment changed the number of genes meeting FDR < 0.05 from 7,898 in frozen W5 to 5,277. 5,015 W5 FDR-significant genes remained significant, corresponding to W5 FDR retention of 0.6350.

For continuous-age adjustment, genome-wide logFC agreement with W5 was Pearson = 0.9656 and Spearman = 0.9509; genome-wide directional concordance was 0.9127. Median absolute change in logFC was 0.0710, the 95th percentile absolute change was 0.4072, and shared-FDR directional concordance was 1.0000.

## Gender sensitivity

Gender adjustment changed the number of genes meeting FDR < 0.05 from 7,898 in frozen W5 to 7,745. 7,596 W5 FDR-significant genes remained significant, corresponding to W5 FDR retention of 0.9618.

For gender adjustment, genome-wide logFC agreement with W5 was Pearson = 0.9946 and Spearman = 0.9965; genome-wide directional concordance was 0.9885. Median absolute change in logFC was 0.0086, the 95th percentile absolute change was 0.1110, and shared-FDR directional concordance was 1.0000.

## Cross-sensitivity interpretation

Continuous-age adjustment produced the larger change in the FDR-significant discovery set: W5 FDR retention was 0.6350 after age adjustment versus 0.9618 after gender adjustment. At the same time, shared-FDR directional concordance was 1.0000 for both sensitivity analyses.

## W7 robustness conclusion

Taken together, the prespecified W7 sensitivity analyses support robustness of the principal frozen W5 IPF-vs-NDC differential-expression effect pattern to the evaluated age and gender adjustments. The FDR-significant discovery set was more sensitive to continuous-age adjustment than to gender adjustment. This distinction does not redefine the frozen W5 primary analysis and does not imply absence of age or gender effects.

## Interpretation boundary

W7 evaluates robustness to the prespecified covariate adjustments. It does not establish that age or gender has no biological or statistical influence.

No W5 or W6 result was recalculated, overwritten, re-ranked, or modified during this interpretation.
